# Reuse permission request — MAPFRE Asset Management

**Status: draft — do not send without review.**

To: MAPFRE Asset Management, S.G.I.I.C., S.A. (compliance/investor-relations contact TBD)

## What votes-es is

`votes-es` is a non-commercial, open-source research dataset and public
reference site (https://votes.h1756.es) publishing normalized,
provenance-backed records of institutional voting at shareholder
meetings of Spanish listed companies. Source code and dataset are on
GitHub (Huntsman1756/votes-es). No ads, no paywall, no data sale.

## What we request

Written permission to:

1. extract, by automated means, the itemized voting records contained in
   MAPFRE AM's annual "Informe de actividades de implicación y ejercicio
   de la política de voto" PDFs published on mapfream.com (the ISS
   "Vote Summary" annex — company, meeting, item, proposal, proposer,
   vote, management recommendation, for/against management);
2. publish **normalized vote facts** (issuer, meeting date, proposal,
   vote direction, management recommendation/alignment) in the votes-es
   dataset, with attribution and a link to the source document;
3. redistribute those normalized facts under the project's open data
   license.

## What we would NOT do

- redistribute the PDF documents themselves or reproduce their text
  wholesale;
- attribute votes to individual MAPFRE funds/mandates — the register is
  published as consolidated MAPFRE AM vote execution and we preserve
  exactly that reporting scope;
- infer or estimate any vote not explicitly recorded;
- imply endorsement by MAPFRE of the project or its analysis.

## Why the records matter

The annex is the only proposal-level public register published directly
on a Spanish SGIIC's own domain. Without permission our publication of
your votes is blocked by your site's general reproduction clause — which
is why we ask explicitly rather than infer.

## Fallback questions if a blanket license is declined

- Would MAPFRE prefer to offer the itemized register as a downloadable
  data file (CSV/XLSX) with explicit reuse terms?
- Is there an existing licensing contact/department for data reuse?
