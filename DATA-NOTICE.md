# DATA NOTICE — votes-es

The code of this repository is MIT-licensed. **The data is not covered by the
MIT license.** Data is sourced from public regulatory disclosures:

- **SEC EDGAR (Form N-PX)**: US public filings; SEC permits access under its
  fair-access policy (declared User-Agent, rate limits).
- **ISS Vote Disclosure Service (VDS)** hosted registers: public disclosure
  pages operated on behalf of asset managers under Ley 35/2003 art. 47 ter.4 /
  SRD II. Redistributed here as **derived facts with provenance** while reuse
  terms remain `PUBLIC_ACCESS_REUSE_UNCLEAR`; we link to the source register
  rather than republish raw payloads.
- Absence of a vote record means **not observed / not disclosed**, never
  "did not vote" or "abstained".
